# jagtapp

Public pages and data for **JagtFokus**, a Danish hunting app for iPhone and
Android.

- `index.html` — support for the iPhone app
- `privatliv.html` — privacy policy for the iPhone app
- `android/` — support and privacy policy for the Android app
- `data/jagttider.json` — the hunting season data both apps download

Both app repos are private. `data/jagttider.json` is edited and tested in the
iPhone repo, then mirrored here by the `Public data` workflow. The `android/`
folder is copied from the Android repo's `docs/` by the `Public pages`
workflow. Do not edit either here: the next mirror overwrites the files.

Pages are served from `main`, `/`: <https://kennethegholm.github.io/jagtapp/>

The app is not the law. Seasons come from BEK nr. 470/2024 and the SGAV hunting
table. You are responsible for hunting legally.
