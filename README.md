# jagtapp

Public pages and data for **JagtFokus**, an iPhone app with Danish hunting
seasons by date and place.

- `index.html` — support
- `privatliv.html` — privacy policy
- `data/jagttider.json` — the hunting season data the app downloads

The app itself is in a private repo. `data/jagttider.json` is edited and tested
there, then mirrored to this repo by the `Public data` workflow. Do not edit it
here: the next mirror overwrites the file.

Pages are served from `main`, `/`: <https://kennethegholm.github.io/jagtapp/>

The app is not the law. Seasons come from BEK nr. 470/2024 and the SGAV hunting
table. You are responsible for hunting legally.
